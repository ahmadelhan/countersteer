Fonts
Both fonts are from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The licence texts are in `fonts/`.
- Russo One by Jovanny Lemonad: headings, buttons and the subtitle (`images/subtitle.png`).
- Dela Gothic One by artakana: the カウンター title (`images/title.png`, `images/title_glow.png`) and the 峠 kanji (`images/kanji.png`).
The title, subtitle and kanji images are rendered from fonts by `tools/make_title.py`.
